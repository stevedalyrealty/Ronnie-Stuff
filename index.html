Smash-style moves
Flick a direction + Attack at the same time = Smash attack (hold to charge).
Hold a direction + Attack = tilt. Attack while running = dash attack.
Attack in the air + direction = aerials. Down-air is a meteor.
On a phone
Drag anywhere on the left half to use the joystick. Right side: A attack, B special, JUMP, SHIELD.
Flick the stick + A at the same time = smash attack. Stick up + A is an up attack. Only the JUMP button jumps.
Start your thumb a little in from the phone's edge so the phone's back-swipe doesn't grab it. Add to Home Screen for the best feel.
Napkin fighters
Spike (goat): Side+B Goat Ram, B Horn Toss, Down+B Hoof Stomp (air dive) or Stomp Quake (ground).
Noodles (orca): Side+B Breach leap, B water Spout, Down+B Belly Flop or Splash.
Pency (pencil): B fires 3 shavings, Side+B slow Bag Swing that kills, Down+B Pencil Drop spike.
Bootisaurus (2x size): B Chomp, Side+B Lace Whip tail sweep, Down+B Boot Stomp. Very hard to launch, very easy to hit.
Baggy (no arms): four jumps and floats, B pulls random junk from the bag, Side+B Bag Roll, Down+B Puff Up.
Snakey (snake cop): B tosses two tickets, Side+B Slither Charge, Down+B Coil Counter: get hit during it and he hits back instead.
Cloudy (cloud, no legs): five jumps, hold JUMP in the air to hover. B Lightning Bolt, Side+B Gust pushes far, Down+B Thunderhead Drop or Rain Dance.
Franchesca (fish with legs): rolls go farther and faster. B Bubble Shot floats up, Side+B Fish Flop hops forward, Down+B Belly Flop or Splash Zone.
Shoean (shoe): fastest runner. B Lace Shot, Side+B Lace Lasso pulls them in, Down+B Heel Drop or Scuff. Run longer before a dash attack for a SPRINT! bonus.
Luke Lava (lava blob): every hit sets you on fire for 2 seconds. Lava can't hurt him. B Fireball, Side+B Lava Flow multi-hit slide, Down+B Meteor Drop or Magma Pool, which leaves a burning puddle under him.
Kayden (ninja knife): hits from behind do 30% more, rolls are shadow steps. B three Shuriken, Side+B Blade Dash, Down+B Smoke Bomb turns him near-invisible.
Po-po (bead cop): bounces when he lands hard. B Donut Toss, Side+B Cuff Toss pulls them in, Down+B Siren Spin.
Larry (giraffe): the longest reach in the game, especially upward. B Neck Snap, Side+B Gallop, Down+B Hoof Drop or Leaf Shower.
Rollo (jailbird rug): Side+B Unroll is a huge flat whip, Down+B Wrap Up pulls them in. B Dust Puff.
Ian (elephant): heaviest fighter, armor on smashes. B Trumpet shoves everything away, Side+B Trunk Grab pulls them in, Down+B Belly Drop or Big Stomp.
Gary (sheep mayor): wool softens every hit he takes by 15%. B Campaign Flyers, Side+B Wool Roll, Down+B Gavel Drop or Town Meeting.
Signature mechanics
Spike: smashes have armor, weak hits won't stop them. Noodles: bites chew shields twice as fast. Pency: land the tip of a stab for a TIP! bonus. Bootisaurus: heavy armor on smashes and Lace Whip. Baggy: Pogo Stomp bounces off whoever it hits. Snakey: Baton Combo chains three hits, Tail Trip pulls the opponent in. Cloudy: hover by holding JUMP, zap moves crackle with lightning. Franchesca: slippery rolls. Shoean: Sprint Kick scales with run-up. Luke Lava: burning hits, immune to lava. Kayden: backstabs. Po-po: bounces. Larry: reach. Rollo: pulls. Ian: armor and weight. Gary: wool armor.
Final Smashes
Every 60 seconds a random fighter glows. Press Special with no direction to unleash it. Lose a stock and you lose it.
Spike Mega Ram · Noodles Tidal Wave · Pency Bank Heist · Bootisaurus Boot Quake · Baggy Trash Tornado · Snakey Full Arrest · Cloudy Super Storm · Franchesca School of Fish · Shoean Marathon Dash · Luke Lava Eruption · Kayden Thousand Blades · Po-po Riot Charge · Larry Tall Order · Rollo Jailbreak · Ian Stampede · Gary Mayor's Parade.
Stages
Sixteen arenas, one home stage per fighter. Rollo's is The Slammer, a jail, because he got caught. Lava Lift is Luke Lava's and a hazard stage: lava rains from the volcano, burns on contact and leaves burning puddles. Luke is immune. Fish Tank is underwater with slow falls and swaying seaweed, Shoebox Stack is a shoe store with a sliding box, Cloud Nine floats in the sky with drifting cloud platforms, Goat Peak has high side ledges, Orca Cove has a drifting boat, Pency's Vault has a platform that rides up and down, Boot Canyon is one giant boot, Baggy's Dump has rolling tires, Snakey's Precinct has patrolling squad cars. Pick Random to shuffle.
Defense & specials
Shield + Left/Right = roll · Shield + Down = spot dodge · Shield in the air = air dodge.
Up + Special = recovery. Special alone = projectile. Side + Special = lunge.
Fall near the edge to grab the ledge. Esc pauses.

Three stocks. Higher damage % = farther launch. Last fighter standing wins. Every minute one random fighter gets a Final Smash: press Special to unleash it. The hat button in the bottom-left puts a hat on everyone, bells and all.
