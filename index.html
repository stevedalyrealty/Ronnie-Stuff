SUPERHAT BROSBROS

A platform fighter. Rack up damage, launch them off the stage, and remember: everyone looks better in a hat.

Smash-style moves
Flick a direction + Attack at the same time = Smash attack (hold to charge).
Hold a direction + Attack = tilt. Attack while running = dash attack.
Attack in the air + direction = aerials. Down-air is a meteor.
On a phone
Drag anywhere on the left half to use the joystick. Right side: A attack, B special, JUMP, SHIELD.
Flick the stick + A at the same time = smash attack. Stick up + A is an up attack. Only the JUMP button jumps.
Start your thumb a little in from the phone's edge so the phone's back-swipe doesn't grab it. Add to Home Screen for the best feel.
Napkin fighters
Spike (goat): Side+B Goat Ram, B Horn Toss, Down+B Hoof Stomp (air dive) or Stomp Quake (ground).
Noodles (orca): Side+B Breach leap, B water Spout, Down+B Belly Flop or Splash.
Pency (pencil): B fires 3 shavings, Side+B slow Bag Swing that kills, Down+B Pencil Drop spike.
Bootisaurus (2x size): B Chomp, Side+B Lace Whip tail sweep, Down+B Boot Stomp. Very hard to launch, very easy to hit.
Baggy (no arms): four jumps and floats, B pulls random junk from the bag, Side+B Bag Roll, Down+B Puff Up.
Snakey (snake cop): B tosses two tickets, Side+B Slither Charge, Down+B Coil Counter: get hit during it and he hits back instead.
Final Smashes
Every 60 seconds a random fighter glows. Press Special with no direction to unleash it. Lose a stock and you lose it.
Rook Disc Storm · Tank Meteor Slam · Fern Overgrowth · Zip Lightning Rush · Spike Mega Ram · Noodles Tidal Wave · Pency Bank Heist · Bootisaurus Boot Quake · Baggy Trash Tornado · Snakey Full Arrest.
Stages
Thirteen arenas. Every napkin fighter has a home stage. Orca Cove has a drifting boat, Snakey's Precinct has patrolling squad cars, Baggy's Dump has rolling tires, Boot Canyon is one giant boot, Pency's Vault and Lava Lift have platforms that ride up and down, Orbit Station slides sideways in low gravity, Bell Tower is tiny with ledges everywhere. Pick Random to shuffle.
Defense & specials
Shield + Left/Right = roll · Shield + Down = spot dodge · Shield in the air = air dodge.
Up + Special = recovery. Special alone = projectile. Side + Special = lunge.
Fall near the edge to grab the ledge. Esc pauses.

Three stocks. Higher damage % = farther launch. Last fighter standing wins. Every minute one random fighter gets a Final Smash: press Special to unleash it. The hat button in the bottom-left puts a hat on everyone, bells and all.
