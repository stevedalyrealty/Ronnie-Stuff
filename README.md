# Super Hat Bros

**Play it free in your browser:** https://stevedalyrealty.github.io/Ronnie-Stuff/

Super Hat Bros is a Smash-style platform fighting game starring sixteen fighters drawn on napkins by a kid named Ronnie: Spike the goat, Noodles the orca, Pency the bank-robbing pencil, Bootisaurus Rex, Baggy, Snakey, Cloudy, Franchesca, Shoean, Luke Lava, Kayden, Po-po, Larry the giraffe, Raggy, Ian the elephant and Mayor Gary. Every fighter has their own moves, Final Smash, home stage and belled hat.

- Works on phones (touch joystick and buttons) and computers (keyboard, two players on one keyboard).
- Online play on two phones: one hosts with a 4-letter code, the other joins.
- Installable as an app (Add to Home Screen) and playable offline.
- Music recorded by Ronnie, with a piano layer that follows his melody.

See [README-game.md](README-game.md) for the full fighter list, controls and how everything works.
