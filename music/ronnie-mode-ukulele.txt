RONNIE-MODE!  (ukulele)
Martial-arts disco theme from Super Hat Bros. Composed for Ronnie, 2026.
Tempo 124 bpm, 4/4. Standard tuning G C E A. Every number is a fret; 0 = open string. Numbers in a row are eighth notes (two per beat).
